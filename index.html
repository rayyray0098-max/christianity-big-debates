Welcome

Christianity includes many different traditions and interpretations of the Bible. Christians share central beliefs, but they do not always agree about how particular passages should be understood or how those beliefs should be applied.

This website explores four major debates. Each page introduces the issue, presents arguments from different Christian viewpoints, and includes biblical references and sources for further reading.

The aim is to explain the disagreements fairly rather than tell readers what they must believe. A Bible reference is included as evidence used in a debate; it does not mean that every Christian interprets it in the same way.

How to use this website

Read both sides of each debate and look up the Bible passages in context. Christians may belong to different denominations, so the views described here are broad positions rather than rules followed by every member of a church.
