4. Can Christians lose their salvation?

Introduction

Christians disagree about whether someone who has genuinely been saved can later lose their salvation. Some believe that a true believer is eternally secure because God keeps them. Others believe that people must continue in faith and can turn away from God. Different churches explain warning passages and promises of security in different ways.

Arguments that Christians cannot lose their salvation

  • Jesus promises security: John 10:27–29 says that Jesus gives his sheep eternal life and that no one can snatch them out of his hand.
  • God completes his work: Philippians 1:6 expresses confidence that God will complete the good work he began in believers.
  • Nothing separates believers from God's love: Romans 8:38–39 says that nothing in creation can separate people from the love of God in Christ.

Arguments that Christians can turn away and lose salvation

  • Warnings against falling away: Hebrews 6:4–6 describes people who have shared in spiritual experiences and then fallen away. Some Christians understand this as a warning that a believer can abandon faith.
  • Continue in faith: Colossians 1:22–23 connects being presented as holy with continuing in faith and not moving away from the hope of the gospel.
  • Remain faithful: Matthew 24:13 says that the one who stands firm to the end will be saved.

Biblical references

  • John 10:27–29: Jesus describes the security of his sheep in his hand and the Father's hand.
  • Romans 8:38–39: Paul says nothing can separate believers from God's love in Christ.
  • Philippians 1:6: Paul expresses confidence that God will finish the work he began.
  • Hebrews 6:4–6: A warning passage about falling away, interpreted differently by Christian traditions.
  • Matthew 24:13: Jesus speaks about standing firm to the end.

Conclusion

The disagreement depends partly on how Christians interpret the Bible's promises of security alongside its warnings about falling away. Those who believe in eternal security emphasise God's power and faithfulness. Those who believe salvation can be lost emphasise the need to continue trusting God. Both positions take seriously the importance of God's grace and a continuing relationship with him.
